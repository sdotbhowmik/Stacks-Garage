Who we are

A software firm built for Bangladesh, by Bangladeshis.

StackGarage was founded in 2020 by a small team of engineers who wanted to bring world-class software engineering practices to the local market — without the international-agency price tag.

Today our 25-strong team designs, develops and operates full-stack applications for SMEs and enterprises across South Asia, the Middle East and beyond.

We believe honest pricing, transparent timelines and 24/7 local support aren't optional extras — they're the baseline. That's the StackGarage promise.
